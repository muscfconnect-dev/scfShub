# Photo attribution

Campus and student photographs included with this project were obtained from Maduka University’s official website on 21 October 2026. The exact observed image URLs and optimized filenames are in `official-photos.json`.

- `mu-garden.webp`: campus garden and paths, from `/images/slider/1.jpg`.
- `mu-hostels.webp`: campus accommodation buildings, from `/images/slider/2.jpg`.
- `mu-buildings.webp`: university buildings, from `/images/slider/3.jpg`.
- `mu-students.webp`: students outdoors, from `/images/main.jpg`; the official site uses this as a general university image.

These photographs provide campus imagery for the approved design. Original food, coffee, library and basketball sample photographs were not present in the uploads and could not be retrieved from the private original preview. CMS uploads can supply verified replacements.

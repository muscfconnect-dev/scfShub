MTH 101Sample review

Elementary Mathematics I

General Studies › General courses · 100 level

3/5Overall experience
Difficulty ChallengingStudy time per week 5–7 hours

Reading the solution isn’t the same as solving it.

I thought I understood a topic until I tried a question on my own. Regular practice helped much more than leaving everything until a few days before the test.

What helped

Try each question yourself first. When you get stuck, note the exact step you couldn’t follow.

CYB 201Sample review

Introduction to Cybersecurity

Computing › Cybersecurity · 200 level

5/5Overall experience
Difficulty ModerateStudy time per week 3–5 hours

Keep asking how it works in real life.

The security concepts were easier to remember when I linked them to everyday situations, like account security and suspicious messages.

What helped

Use the examples from class to test your understanding, then practise with revision questions.

SEN 201Sample review

Introduction to Software Engineering

Computing › Software Engineering · 200 level

4/5Overall experience
Difficulty ModerateStudy time per week 3–5 hours

The examples make it click.

There are quite a few terms and processes to understand. Connecting them to an app you already use makes the work feel much less abstract.

What helped

Work through the development stages with one small project in mind.

COS 101Sample review

Introduction to Computing

Computing › Computer Science · 100 level

4/5Overall experience
Difficulty ManageableStudy time per week 3–5 hours

Get the basics right early.

The topics build on each other, so it helps to understand the first few weeks properly. I found the practical examples easier to follow than just reading definitions.

What helped

Make short notes after class and try explaining each topic without looking at them.
